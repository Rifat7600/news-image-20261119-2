One image summing up the world's news of Thursday 19 November 2026: a
world map with numbered pins for the day's top stories — the first-ever
Trump–Xi–Putin trilateral in Shenzhen, the Iran war widening across the
Gulf, Israel's Gaza ground operation, Cyclone Akvan nearing the Andhra
coast, William and Kate meeting Modi in Delhi, Hakeem Jeffries nominated
US House Speaker, and Anthropic's IPO debut on the Nasdaq.

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) copies `entry.png` to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png`. `generate.py` is the Pillow script that
drew the image from the verified facts of the day.
